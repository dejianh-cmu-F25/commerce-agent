# Warranty

## Coverage

All outdoor gear carries a one-year manufacturer warranty against defects in
materials and workmanship. The warranty term starts on the delivery date.

## What is covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as broken zippers, failed seams,
and pole failures. If a seam comes apart under normal use, it is a covered
defect.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, or accidental damage.
Damage caused by an accident is not a warranty claim.

## How to make a claim

Contact support with your order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims
are repaired or replaced at no cost; the customer does not pay for warranty
shipping.

## Warranty is not a return

A warranty claim is not a return. Warranty covers defects over the one-year term;
a return is for a change of mind within the return window (see returns.md).
