# Amazon shipping & delivery (sourced)

> Sourced from Amazon's public "Shipping and Delivery" help page (paraphrased with
> a citation; no copyrighted text copied verbatim). Shipping is **informational** —
> it is not part of the return-decision policy, so it is not in the policy SoT.

Source: [Amazon Shipping and Delivery](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468520)
— retrieved 2023-06-25 (Internet Archive capture).

Amazon's shipping help explains how to check an order's status and what to do about
late or undeliverable orders. The main topics are:

- **Order tracking** — use *Your Orders* to find tracking information and delivery details.
- **Missing package that shows as delivered** — report it and follow Amazon's missing-package process.
- **Late deliveries** — the process for a package that is past its estimated delivery date.
- **Missing item from a package** — report a partial delivery and follow the process.
- **Shipping rates and times** — how to determine the cost and speed for an order.
- **Undeliverable packages** — what happens when a package cannot be delivered.
- **Prime shipping** — Prime FREE Same-Day Delivery and Prime shipping benefits.
- **General shipping policies** — delivery guarantees and general shipping information.
- **Special shipping options** — large items and heavy/bulky services, collection points.
- **International shipping** — global shipping rates and international orders/deliveries.
