# Agent Note: Measured results live in the specs and are gate-checked

Status: implemented (2026-09-13) — process

## Problem

The project's evaluation numbers lived only in `evals/report.md`, which is
regenerated and easy to overlook. The spec (P1: the source of truth) did not
state what the features actually achieved, so a reviewer (or a resume) could not
see "we changed X and measured Y" without digging into the report. Numbers that
are not recorded, and not checked, drift.

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave the numbers in the report only.** The report is regenerated and not a
  specification; the specs would keep describing intent without evidence.
  Rejected.
- **Hand-copy numbers into the specs, unchecked.** Cheap, but the specs drift
  from the report the moment a benchmark changes. Rejected.
- **Embed the whole report in the specs.** Duplicates a large artifact and
  couples the specs to its formatting. Rejected.
- **A canonical `specs/RESULTS.md` plus per-spec slices, checked in the gate.**
  Chosen: one authoritative record, each spec carries its own slice, and a check
  fails when the keyless numbers diverge.

## Decision

- **`specs/RESULTS.md` is the canonical record.** It holds the retrieval
  benchmark, the feature ablation, the real-model reliability/process/cost/judge
  numbers, the deployment smoke, and the quality gate, with provenance (model,
  seeds, date) and the reproduce commands.
- **Each relevant spec carries a `## Measured Results` section** with its slice
  and a link to the canonical record. Success criteria are backed by measured
  values, not just intent.
- **The gate checks the keyless numbers.** `scripts/check_results.py` compares
  `specs/RESULTS.md` against the freshly generated `evals/results-keyless.json`
  and fails on divergence.
- **Real-model numbers are dated snapshots.** They are recorded with the model,
  seeds, and date, and explicitly marked as not regenerated by the gate.

## Consequences

- The specs now state measured outcomes; a reviewer sees the effect of each
  change without leaving the specification.
- The keyless sections cannot silently drift; the real sections can become stale,
  which is why they are dated and labelled.
- Updating a result means regenerating the report and updating the canonical
  record; the check keeps the two honest.
- This is a process decision, not a feature; it is recorded here per DR-1.
