# Returns policy (v2 — active)

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to orders placed on or after 2026-01-01. The
previous policy is archived in returns-v1.md and must not be used for decisions.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The 30-day window starts
on the delivery date, not the order date. Orders delivered more than 30 days ago
are outside the window unless an exception below applies.

## Condition

Items must be unused and unopened, with original tags and packaging. An item that
has been opened, worn, or damaged by the customer may be refused or charged a
restocking fee.

## Non-returnable items

Final-sale items and gift cards cannot be returned. Intimate apparel and
hazardous materials are also non-returnable. These are marked on the product page
at checkout.

## Restocking fee

A 15% restocking fee applies to non-defective returns of large items such as tents
and furniture. The restocking fee is waived when the item is defective, arrived
damaged, or was the wrong item.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free when the item is defective, damaged, or incorrectly
shipped. In all other cases the customer pays return shipping, which is deducted
from the refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
after we receive and inspect the return. Original shipping charges are not
refunded unless the item was defective.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for a different size or color, subject to stock.
Replacement items ship once the returned item is received.

## Exceptions

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items are accepted outside the standard 30-day
window and without a restocking fee. Contact support with photos of the issue.

## How to start a return

Contact support with your order number and the item you want to return. Support
creates a return request; once it is approved you receive a prepaid return label
when return shipping is covered.
