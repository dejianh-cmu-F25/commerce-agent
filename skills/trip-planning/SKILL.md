# Trip planning

1. Establish the trip length, season, and budget (use what the customer said or
   what you remember; ask only if it is genuinely missing).
2. Group the needs: shelter, sleep, cooking, water, and carrying.
3. Search the catalog for each group; prefer in-stock items and note the price.
4. Add the chosen items to the cart and render a checkout summary.
5. Keep the total at or under the budget and state the total plainly.
