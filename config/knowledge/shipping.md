# Shipping policy

## Options and cost

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $6. Orders over $75 ship
free within the continental United States. Free shipping does not apply to Alaska,
Hawaii, or international destinations.

Expedited shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days for $18. Expedited orders
placed before 2pm local time ship the same day.

## Delivery and weekends

Standard delivery runs Monday through Friday; standard orders do not arrive on
weekends. Expedited orders placed before 2pm on Friday can arrive over the
weekend. The estimated delivery date (EDD) shown at checkout accounts for
weekends and holidays.

## Tracking and exceptions

Tracking is emailed when the order leaves the warehouse. If tracking has not
updated for more than 3 business days, contact support.

We do not ship expedited orders to PO boxes. If a shipment is marked delivered
but the customer did not receive it, support opens a carrier trace before any
replacement is shipped.
